## Eduardo Potumati

I am a **Senior Software Engineer and Software Architect** with over 20 years of experience designing, building, modernizing, and operating production systems.

I started working with technology at a young age. At 14, I helped put a Brazilian rural TV channel on the internet, streaming continuously over dial-up-era connections. Since then, my career has expanded across software architecture, rural marketplaces, online auctions, pet adoption platforms, database performance, cloud infrastructure, and low-latency streaming.

My primary focus is software architecture: understanding complex business problems, making pragmatic technical decisions, and building systems that remain maintainable as both traffic and requirements evolve.

### What I Build

My experience covers the complete lifecycle of production software, from architecture and implementation to infrastructure, performance tuning, modernization, and long-term maintenance.

I have worked extensively with:

* **.NET and C#:** ASP.NET Core, Web APIs, MVC, background processing, concurrency, Native AOT, and application modernization.
* **Software Architecture:** Distributed systems, Design Patterns, Domain-Driven Design (DDD), Clean Architecture, Clean Code, SOLID principles, and event-driven architectures.
* **Databases:** SQL Server, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Entity Framework Core, query optimization, concurrency control, indexing, and high-volume data processing.
* **Cloud and Infrastructure:** Azure Functions, Service Bus, App Service, AWS Lambda, EventBridge, S3, EC2, CI/CD, observability, and production operations.
* **Real-Time Systems:** SignalR, online auction platforms, asynchronous pipelines, and low-latency media delivery.

### Engineering Approach

I see software development as an engineering discipline that requires rigor without losing pragmatism.

Architecture should support the business instead of becoming an end in itself. I apply DDD, SOLID principles, Clean Code, and Design Patterns when they make a system clearer and easier to evolve, not simply because a diagram or framework says they should be there.

I also believe that performance work begins with evidence. Whether the problem involves database locks, memory pressure, query plans, application concurrency, or streaming latency, I prefer measuring the real system, identifying the root cause, and understanding the trade-offs before choosing a solution.

### Selected Work

* **Canal do Boi:** I helped launch and evolve one of Brazil's earliest full-time internet TV operations, following its technical journey from RealServer and dial-up connections to HLS, cloud infrastructure, and WebRTC with sub-second latency.
* **MF Rural:** I participated in building and scaling one of Brazil's largest rural marketplaces, from its original Classic ASP and SQL Server architecture to modern .NET systems.
* **Online Auction Platforms:** I have maintained platforms that facilitated more than **1 million bids** and over **R$350 million in sales**, combining business-critical software with real-time interaction and streaming.
* **Adotar.com.br:** I developed and continue to maintain a major Brazilian pet rescue and adoption platform, using technology to connect animals, shelters, volunteers, and adopters.

### This Blog

This blog is where I document practical lessons from real engineering work: architectural decisions, production incidents, performance investigations, database behavior, concurrency, artificial intelligence, and the evolution of systems that have remained online for years.

The goal is not to present technology as a collection of perfect solutions. It is to explain what worked, what failed, and which trade-offs mattered in practice.
